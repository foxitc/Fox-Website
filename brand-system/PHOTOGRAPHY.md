# PHOTOGRAPHY

Status: **PROPOSED / staging**. Placeholder imagery in all previews is abstract — real photography is an owner input.

- **Style:** authentic Fox ITC staff, real UK venues/sites and genuine customer settings. Natural light, premium but approachable, people-first. No stock "AI circuit-brain", fake handshakes, invented customers or staged clichés.
- **The arc photo window:** one photo shows through the exact arc masks (`mode="photo"`); set a per-arc focal point so the subject reads at small sizes. If content isn't recognisable small, use the solid treatment instead.
- **Treatment:** subtle depth; keep essential subject matter clear of the arc gaps. Never bake essential text into a photo — keep it as live HTML/vector.
- **Rights & people:** licensed images only; don't present AI-generated people as real staff/customers; get consent for identifiable people.
- **Specs:** landscape heroes ≥ 2400px wide; square social ≥ 1080px; print photos 300dpi at final size. Optimise/resize; set width/height; lazy-load below the fold.
- **Shot list to supply:** team at work, office interior/exterior, support in action, a hospitality venue, a warehouse/logistics scene.
