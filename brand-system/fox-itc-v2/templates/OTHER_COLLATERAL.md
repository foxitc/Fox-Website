# Additional reusable brand surfaces

Business cards, letterheads, quotations, reports, audits, customer onboarding packs, proposal covers, case-study PDFs, event signage, pull-up banners, vehicle decals, service status pages, onboarding emails and digital signage. For each, choose one of three treatments: exact arc accent, exact arc watermark, or exact arc photographic window. Preserve official logo clearance and typography. Design to the actual supplier's dimensions, print requirements and viewing distance. No unsupported client proof or unapproved product names.
