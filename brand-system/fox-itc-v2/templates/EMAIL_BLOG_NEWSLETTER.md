# Email signatures, newsletters, blog and email banners

## Outlook signature
Table-based HTML, inline CSS, system-safe fallbacks, modest width (~500–600px), accessible live text for name/job/phone/web, official logo linked to homepage, optional small arc motif as an image with dimensions/alt. No animation dependency, no image-only signature, test Outlook desktop/web/mobile and Gmail. Personal data populated from approved directory.

## Email banner
Typical working canvas 1200×400 or responsive 600×200 logical px; arc crop to left/right, 1 short headline, no essential text baked into photos; mobile-safe CTA in live HTML. Always provide fallback background.

## Customer newsletter
Subject/preheader, intro, 2–4 story modules, verified service updates, one main CTA, footer/contact/unsubscribe where legally required. Arc watermark in header, not repeated behind each article. UK GDPR/PECR review for marketing consent and unsubscribes.

## Blog and Fox Bytes LinkedIn newsletter
Editorial cover with consistent masthead, topic imagery in one arc treatment, reading-friendly article width (~680–760px proposed), H2/H3 structure, key takeaways, sourced claims, relevant existing-service CTA, author/date and related posts. Avoid AI filler and unsupported facts.
