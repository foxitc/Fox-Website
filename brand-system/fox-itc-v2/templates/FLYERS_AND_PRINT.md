# Flyers, brochures, catalogues, beer mats and print

## A5 flyer (148 × 210 mm) and A4 (210 × 297 mm)
Use 3 mm bleed all sides (printer to confirm), ~5 mm minimum safe margin, CMYK/proofing as printer specifies, 300 dpi photos at final size, print-ready PDF and editable source. Front: concise customer outcome headline, one authentic photo framed by exact arcs, 3 proof/service points, one primary CTA and QR code with human-readable URL. Back: service catalogue or approved managed support tier table, contact details, source-backed benefits. Avoid tiny type and visual clutter.

## Rate cards and proposals
Clear hierarchy, readable tables, terms and exclusions, pricing sourced from approved catalogue, date/version, QR or booking CTA. Use exact arc watermark on cover, not behind dense tables.

## Beer mats
Produce square and round versions only after printer die-line dimensions are known. Keep artwork within safe area; avoid detailed text at edge. Side A: bold human-facing message + official wordmark + oversized arc motif. Side B: short hospitality-relevant offer, one QR and readable URL, contact. Use solid or photo arcs where legible; avoid unverified 'free' or discount claims.

## Quality
Check bleed, trim, overprint, minimum QR physical size (~20–25 mm suggested, scan-test actual), contrast, embedded fonts, no raster-upscaled 236px source on large format. Use approved vector for big print runs.
