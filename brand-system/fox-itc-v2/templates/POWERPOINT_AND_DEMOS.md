# PowerPoint, sales presentations, web demos, video

## PowerPoint 16:9
Canvas 13.333 × 7.5 in. Master layouts: (1) cover with huge arc crop + title; (2) agenda; (3) section divider with opposite arc orientation; (4) problem/solution split; (5) services overview; (6) industry use case; (7) process/timeline; (8) data/chart; (9) genuine case study; (10) team; (11) proposal/pricing; (12) closing/contact. Use editable text and shapes, real charts where possible, restrained animations, 28–36pt titles and readable body copy; don't put text on busy photos. Include slide numbers/footer and master theme colours. Do not rasterise whole slides as images.

## Web demo / SaaS presentation
Responsive demo landing shell: branded header, product name, navigation, screenshot/browser-frame area, use-case sidebar, step indicator, guided highlights and clear exit/next step. Use real app screenshots or clearly labelled prototypes; never invent product capabilities. Arc watermark outside interactive UI to avoid obscuring controls. Show Fox ITC wordmark as parent brand where relevant.

## Demo video and motion
Intro: 3-arc pulse (inner→outer), product title, short transition; screen capture with discreet corner brand motif; lower-third label; outro with official logo/CTA. Deliver 16:9 and 9:16 variants, silent-safe captions, audio rights, reduced-motion alternative. Do not morph arcs into new shapes. Recommended 5–8 second intro maximum unless brief specifies otherwise.

## QA
Presentations editable and projector-safe; demo UI accessible, keyboard friendly and responsive; video not falsely representing live functionality; all screenshots/claims validated.
