# LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook and social

## Master variants
LinkedIn single image 1200×1200 (square) or 1200×627 (landscape); Instagram feed 1080×1350 (4:5), square 1080×1080, stories 1080×1920; LinkedIn carousel 1080×1350 per slide as working artboard, export platform-appropriate PDF/image. Verify current channel specs at publishing time.

## Content families
1. Practical tips: bold insight, short explanation, supporting icon, discreet arc watermark.
2. Customer story: verified quote/result, authentic photo inside arc mask, source and CTA.
3. Hospitality/freight sector insight: industry photography, sector problem, service connection, CTA.
4. Team culture: genuine employee photo, authentic quote, solid/orange arc corner.
5. Fox Bytes newsletter cover: recurring title lockup, edition/date, one topic illustration, oversized crop of arcs.
6. Carousel: hook → problem → practical steps → result → CTA, consistent footer and pagination.

## Rules
One idea per frame; no paragraph-heavy cards; headline typically 4–12 words, supporting copy brief, high contrast. Use actual logo; arc position/orientation varies between posts but geometry never changes. Captions in UK English with a useful takeaway, accessible alt text and appropriate CTA. Avoid unsupported urgency and fabricated engagement statistics.
