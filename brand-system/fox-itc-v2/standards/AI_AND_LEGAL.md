# AI production governance

Using AI to assist with coding or layout does not by itself generally require an AI label on an ordinary website under the EU AI Act. Obligations may apply to AI chatbot interactions, deepfake-style media, and specified public-interest text; territorial scope and other law require case-by-case review. Do not claim this file is legal advice.

Do not remove provenance metadata to evade detection. Label synthetic depictions where required and do not pass AI people off as real staff or customers. Verify rights to stock, photos, typefaces and customer marks. Human editorial sign-off is required for public claims, new case studies, legal/privacy language and any regulated statements. Respect UK GDPR/PECR for forms, cookies and campaign tracking. AI can autonomously prepare drafts, variants and QA reports; publication requires explicit deployment permission and approved facts/assets.
