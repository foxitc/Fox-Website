# Brand foundations — controlled draft

## Established inputs
- Business name: Fox ITC
- Main site: https://foxitc.co.uk
- Orange: #FB471B (owner-established)
- Slate: #444B53 (owner-established secondary)
- Fox logo and original three arcs: supplied brand assets; shape is locked.
- Company values from previous owner direction: Simplify IT / Do IT / Care IT / Own IT (verify latest live wording before publication).
- Existing service pages and content are not to be rewritten as part of UI redesign.

## Proposed direction
Premium, confident, approachable, people-first, outcome-led. White/light backgrounds, carefully used charcoal sections, generous whitespace, strong hierarchy, restrained orange. Authentic staff, venues and customer photography. Consistent SVG line icon family. No fake customers, generic AI circuit-brain imagery, faux handwriting, invented testimonials or cartoon overload.

## Typography (not approved)
Candidate headline/body families: Inter or Manrope; inspect actual current font and preserve if appropriate. Responsive type scale, minimum 16px body, strong contrast and accessible focus states. Do not silently switch official logo lettering.

## Voice
Clear British English, plainspoken and helpful. Lead with customer problems and outcomes. Avoid invented metrics and unsupported superlatives. Keep services factual and source-linked.
