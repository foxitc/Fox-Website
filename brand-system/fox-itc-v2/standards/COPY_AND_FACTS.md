# Copy, facts and sector relevance

- Content source priority: approved current website CMS / service catalogue > approved CRM case studies > owner-provided copy > research requiring verification. Do not manufacture client names, statistics, certifications, prices or customer quotes.
- Preserve current site copy during UI-only changes. New industry landing pages may have *proposed* sector-specific copy that requires editorial approval before publishing.
- Generic homepage addresses all target SMB sectors. Industry landing pages address the sector's operational challenges and link to the same existing services, never create a parallel conflicting service catalogue.
- Hospitality: venue operations, EPOS, guest Wi-Fi, connectivity, networking, audio, CCTV and multi-site support only where actually offered.
- Freight/logistics: multi-site support, warehouse Wi-Fi, secure M365, mobile teams, business continuity, connectivity only where actually offered.
- Claims about downtime reduction, uptime percentages, ROI, security certifications, customer numbers and '24/7' support need approved evidence.
- CTA examples: 'Talk to our team', 'Explore our services', 'Book a conversation'; adjust to approved existing conversion flows.
- Accessibility: avoid copy embedded in photographs; put essential information as selectable HTML or editable text.
