The problem

AI is already in your business. The evidence isn't.

Staff are already using AI

Often on personal accounts, pasting in customer and company information without a second thought.

Article 4 of the EU AI Act has applied since 2 February 2025, and it can reach UK organisations whose AI use touches the EU.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does the EU AI Act apply to a UK business?

It can, if you offer AI-supported services in the EU, have staff or customers there, or your AI outputs are used there. Many UK organisations choose to follow it anyway because it's the clearest standard for AI training. The course explains this in plain English.

Isn't there already free government AI training?

Yes, and it's worth doing. Fox AI Licence adds what an employer needs: training on your own tools and policy, records and renewals, and an Evidence Pack you can hand to a customer or insurer.

How long does it take?

About an hour for Level 1, in short lessons that can be done on a phone.

What happens when the rules or tools change?

We update the course, and anyone affected is asked to complete only what's changed.

Can staff without a work email take part?

Yes. Managers can print single-use access codes.

Who can see staff answers and reflections?

Managers see progress and results. Private reflections stay private to the learner.
