What is Cyber Essentials?

Cyber Essentials is a UK government-backed cyber security certification scheme. It helps organisations demonstrate that they have five fundamental technical controls in place to defend against common internet-based attacks. It's a useful baseline — not a guarantee that a business cannot be attacked.

01 · Firewalls

Control traffic between your devices and untrusted networks.

02 · Secure configuration

Remove unnecessary features, change insecure defaults and configure devices safely.

03 · Security update management

Keep operating systems, applications and firmware updated against known weaknesses.

04 · User access control

Give people only the access they need, protect accounts and manage administrator privileges.

05 · Malware protection

Use appropriate protections to help stop malicious software.

What is the Cyber Essentials First Step Fund?

Launched on 3 November 2026, the First Step Fund provides eligible UK micro, small and medium-sized organisations with 75% off the Cyber Essentials assessment fee. Certification is awarded only if the assessment is passed. This is a discount on the assessment and potential certification — not a grant paid into your bank account.

Who qualifies?

The fund is aimed at UK micro, small and medium-sized organisations seeking a new certificate, including those that have not held Cyber Essentials in the last four years. Routine renewals are not eligible. Eligibility and any subsidy rules are checked through the official application process.

When does the funding end?

Eligible organisations must register and purchase their assessment by 31 March 2027, or earlier if funding runs out. IASME states that you then have six months to complete and submit the assessment.

Is there a separate 100% funded option?

Yes. The Cyber Resilience Pledge: Cyber Essentials for Suppliers Grant is a separate scheme for selected SME suppliers nominated by organisations that have signed the Cyber Resilience Pledge. It can provide fully funded Cyber Essentials certification plus support from an NCSC-assured Cyber Advisor. It is not an open grant that any business can claim directly. The government says this scheme runs until March 2029.

Cyber Essentials vs Cyber Essentials Plus

Standard Cyber Essentials uses an independently reviewed self-assessment. Cyber Essentials Plus adds independent technical verification of the same five controls. The First Step Fund specifically covers the standard Cyber Essentials assessment; do not assume that Plus or IT remediation is included.

Frequently asked questions

Does the discount guarantee certification?

No. Your organisation must meet the requirements and pass the assessment.

Can existing Cyber Essentials holders use it?

Normal renewals are not eligible. Organisations whose last certification was more than four years ago may qualify, subject to the official criteria.

Can I apply without an IT provider?

Yes. You can apply through IASME. An IT partner can help you prepare, but technical support is separate from the discounted assessment fee.

How do I apply?

Check the eligibility details at IASME's official First Step Fund page, then register and purchase through the official process or an approved Certification Body.
