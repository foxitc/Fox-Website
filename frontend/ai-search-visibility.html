The problem

Search is becoming a single AI answer

The old game was ranking in a list of ten blue links. The new game is being named in the one answer an AI assistant gives — and if it doesn't know or trust you, you're simply not in the conversation.

One answer, not ten links

AI assistants summarise the web into a single recommendation. Being on page one no longer guarantees you're seen.

If AI can't cite you, you're invisible

These tools quote sources they can read, understand and trust. Content they can't parse — or can't verify — gets left out.

Your competitors may already be the answer

When the AI names a provider and it isn't you, that lead never even reaches your website to be counted.

Questions

Frequently asked

What is AI search visibility?

AI search visibility is how often, and how accurately, AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews mention and recommend your business when people ask them questions. It's also called Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) or Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO). Instead of ranking a list of links, these tools give one answer — so being named in that answer matters.

How is this different from SEO?

Traditional SEO aims to rank your pages in a list of search results. AI search visibility aims to get your business cited inside the single answer an AI assistant gives. The two overlap — clear, factual, well-structured content helps both — but AI engines reward content they can quote and trust, and they rely heavily on structured data. We focus on being the source the AI draws from.

Which AI engines do you measure?

The major public AI answer engines — including ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews — using the kinds of questions your customers actually ask. We report where you appear, where your competitors appear, and where the gaps are.

Can you guarantee we'll be the answer AI gives?

No honest provider can guarantee what a given AI model will say — the models change and we don't control them. What we can do is measure your current visibility, improve the content and signals the engines rely on, and track the change over time so you can see progress.

How long until we see a difference?

It varies by how much content and structured data you already have and how competitive your sector is. AI engines re-read the web continually, so improvements can show within weeks, but building durable authority is an ongoing programme rather than a one-off fix.
